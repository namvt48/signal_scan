AMENDMENT 2 — FOMO merged INTO the "Tracked by" column (user-requested, 2026-09-29)
=====================================================================================
Requester instruction (verbatim): "gộp fomo BY vào chung với tracked by đi các trường
dữ liệu giống nhau mà" then "tạm thời để trade đi, và Buy $ | Sell PnL chốt như này".

SHAPE CHOSEN (option 1 of 3 offered): ONE column, TWO stacked lists.
  The separate "FOMO by" column is deleted. Its sub-table now renders inside the
  "Tracked by" cell, below the wallet sub-table, separated by a top border.
  Both lists keep their own column sets — nothing is lost, nothing is renamed:
    wallet list : Wallet | Clan? | Bal | TXs | Inflow | Age
    FOMO list   : User   | Clan? | Trades | Buy $ | Sell PnL | Age
  Column-count sites: FOMO_EXTRA 1 -> 0 (15 columns with FOMO on, was 16).
  Width: merged cell is w-96 (384px) when SHOW_FOMO is off — INSTANCE A PARITY —
         and w-[768px] (the old tracked 384px + old fomo 384px) when on, so the
         outer min-width stays 2312px and no other column changes size.

FILES
  src/components/SignalTable.tsx   FOMO_EXTRA=0; merged <Th>/<Td>; FOMO label row moved
                                   into the merged header, guarded by `SHOW_FOMO && hasFomo`
                                   (the SHOW_FOMO guard is REQUIRED: `hasFomo` alone is a
                                   runtime prop, so minification cannot drop the FOMO
                                   strings from the OFF bundle — see gate below).
  src/services/dataStore.ts        DEV-ONLY mock FOMO seed (see "not part of the feature").
